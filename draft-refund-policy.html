Refund Policy

1. Beta / Concierge MVP Purchases

Beta / Concierge MVP purchases are refundable in full within 7 days of purchase, provided no custom weekly meal plan has been delivered to you.

2. After Delivery of a Custom Plan

Once a personalized meal plan, grocery list, pantry plan, or savings reset has been delivered, the purchase is generally non-refundable because the work has been customized for the customer, except where a refund is required by applicable law, required by the applicable payment platform, or expressly approved by Studio1261 LLC.

  • Date charging begins: unless you cancel before the trial ends, your first subscription charge occurs when the 14-day trial period ends.

Canceling before the trial ends means you will not be charged.

4. Subscriptions

Subscription plans automatically renew at the end of each billing period until you cancel. You may cancel at any time before the next billing date to stop future charges; cancellation is at least as easy as signing up — no retention call, extra step, or different channel is required beyond what signup used. Cancellation does not retroactively refund payments already made for the current or prior billing periods, and we do not provide prorated refunds for the unused portion of a billing period, except as required by law or as described below.

How to cancel, by purchase channel:

  • Apple App Store subscription: cancel through your Apple ID subscription settings (on iPhone/iPad: Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions, or the subscription's App Store listing). Apple's auto-renewable subscriptions continue renewing until you cancel there — Studio1261 cannot cancel an Apple subscription on your behalf.
  • Direct DinnerNavAI subscription (purchased directly from us, not through Apple): cancel through your DinnerNavAI account/subscription page, or by contacting us at the email below.

Deleting the DinnerNavAI app, or deleting your DinnerNavAI account, does not necessarily cancel an active subscription — you must cancel through the channel above that matches how you subscribed.

5. Purchases Through Third-Party App Stores

Purchases made through the Apple App Store or another third-party marketplace are subject to that marketplace's billing, cancellation, and refund rules, not this Refund Policy. Refund requests for App Store purchases must generally be submitted to Apple directly — currently through Apple's Report a Problem process (reportaproblem.apple.com) — not to Studio1261. DinnerNavAI cannot guarantee approval of a refund requested from a third-party marketplace. Purchases made directly through DinnerNavAI (outside a third-party marketplace) are governed by the rest of this Refund Policy.

If you believe you were charged in error (for example, a duplicate charge or wrong amount), please contact us promptly, preferably within 14 days after discovering a suspected duplicate charge, incorrect amount, or other billing error, at the email below. Nothing in this policy limits rights you may have under applicable law or through your payment provider. We will investigate and issue a corrected charge or refund if an error is confirmed.
