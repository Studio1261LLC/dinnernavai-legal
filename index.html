1. Who We Are

DinnerNavAI (“Service,” “we,” “us,” “our”) is operated by Studio1261 LLC, with its principal place of business at 731 SE Alices Rd #1064, Waukee, IA 50263. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Name and email address
  • Zip code (used to find local grocery pricing and promotions)
  • Household member details: name, age band (adult/child), meals needed, dietary pattern, and any foods/ingredients to avoid
  • Guest details for planned meals (name, day, and foods to avoid), if you add guests
  • Pantry items (name, quantity, unit, storage location, and use-by date)
  • Cooking-schedule preferences (available cook time, activity nights, meal themes)
  • Allergies and dietary preferences or restrictions
  • Meal ratings and feedback on delivered meal plans
  • Feedback and support communications
  • Payment-related account details (processed by our payment processor — see “Payment Information” below)

Information collected automatically:

  • The DinnerNavAI app does not currently use cookies, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting tools, and does not collect device identifiers.
  • Your authentication session is stored securely on your device and used to authenticate requests to our servers.
  • If DinnerNavAI operates a companion website, that website may separately collect browser/device type, pages viewed, approximate location from IP address, and cookies — see “Cookies and Tracking Technologies” below for what applies there.

Payment information: we use a third-party payment processor to handle transactions. We do not store full credit card numbers on our own systems; the processor handles that data under its own privacy and security practices.

3. Sensitive Information

Some of the information you provide — such as allergies, dietary restrictions, or other health-related dietary information — may reveal information about a health condition or, in some cases, a religious belief (for example, a religious dietary practice). Depending on what you provide and applicable law, this may be treated as sensitive personal information.

We use allergy, dietary restriction, health-related dietary, and similar sensitive information only to personalize the Service, apply relevant food restrictions, provide customer support, and operate related Service functions. We do not use allergy or health-related dietary information for targeted advertising or sponsored-product targeting.

4. How We Use Information

  • Generate meal plans, grocery lists, pantry suggestions, and savings estimates
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries
  • Process payments and manage subscriptions
  • Improve and develop the Service
  • Communicate with you about your account, the Service, and (where you have not opted out) related offers

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate the Service, including payment processors, email and customer-communication tools, form and intake tools, analytics providers, AI/automation providers that help generate meal plans, and hosting/infrastructure providers. These providers may only use your information to perform services on our behalf. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

Information necessary to generate meal plans, grocery lists, and related recommendations may be processed by approved AI and automation service providers on our behalf. For sensitive information (such as allergy, dietary restriction, or other health-related dietary information — see §3), we send it only to approved enterprise or API-level AI/automation providers operating under appropriate contractual, privacy, and security terms, not to general consumer-facing AI tools or personal AI accounts.

Affiliate retailers and sponsors do not receive identifiable household, pantry, allergy, health-related dietary, or meal-preference information merely because they sponsor or participate in a recommendation. See our Affiliate & Sponsored Disclosure for more on how sponsored and affiliate relationships work.

The DinnerNavAI app itself does not use cookies. If DinnerNavAI operates a companion website, that website and its service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember preferences, and understand how it is used; you would be able to control cookies through your browser settings there. 7. Data Retention

How long we retain personal information depends on the type of information, the purpose for which it was collected, your account status, legal and tax requirements, and security/fraud-prevention needs. We maintain an internal retention schedule covering categories including your active profile, a closed account, sensitive dietary/allergy information, pantry and meal-plan history, support records, privacy-request records, backups, and payment/accounting records. When information is no longer needed, we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it, consistent with FTC guidance on data minimization.

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Depending on your state of residence and applicable law, you may have some or all of the following rights. To exercise any of them, contact us at the address below:

  • Access / Confirm Processing: confirm whether we are processing your personal information.
  • Obtain a Copy: request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Delete: request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal, tax, fraud-prevention, and payment-dispute exceptions.

We will respond to verified requests consistent with applicable law.

10. Children's Privacy

DinnerNavAI accounts are intended for adults. Children under 13 may not create accounts or directly provide personal information to DinnerNavAI. A parent or legal guardian may provide limited information about a child when necessary to create household meal plans — such as the child's dietary preferences, vegetarian or vegan status, allergies, disliked foods, and school lunch needs — through the parent's own account. This is different from a child directly using the Service or submitting information themselves, which we do not permit. If we learn that a child under 13 has created an account or directly submitted personal information without going through a parent or legal guardian, we will delete that information. COPPA applies to online services directed to children under 13, and to general-audience services with actual knowledge that they are collecting personal information directly from a child under 13 — contact us immediately if you believe this has occurred.
