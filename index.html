Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

1. Who We Are

DinnerNavAI (“Service,” “we,” “us,” “our”) is operated by Studio1261 LLC, with its principal place of business at 731 SE Alices Rd #1064, Waukee, IA 50263. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide in the app:

  • Account details: your email address and password (passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider)
  • Household member details: first name or nickname, age group (adult, teen, child, toddler), dietary pattern, allergies and foods/ingredients to avoid, and protein preferences
  • Meal preferences: eating styles, favorite and rejected meals, favorite recipe sites and recipes
  • Schedule preferences: planning day and cooking time limits
  • Kitchen inventory: food sources (such as pantry or freezer) and items with quantity, unit, and use-by date
  • Meal plans, grocery lists, saved recipes, and meal history
  • Meal ratings, feedback, and issue reports you send about a meal plan
  • Support messages you send us

Information collected automatically:

  • Two first-party usage events, stored on our own servers: when you finish household setup and when a meal plan is generated. We use these only to understand whether the Service is working.
  • Your sign-in session, stored securely in your device’s Keychain and used to authenticate requests to our servers.
  • Standard server logs (such as IP address and request time) kept by our hosting provider for security and troubleshooting.

The DinnerNavAI app does not use advertising, cookies, third-party analytics, or crash-reporting tools, does not collect device advertising identifiers, and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. DinnerNavAI does not currently offer in-app purchases or collect payment information.

3. Sensitive Information

Some of the information you provide — such as allergies, dietary restrictions, or other health-related dietary information — may reveal information about a health condition or, in some cases, a religious belief (for example, a religious dietary practice). Depending on applicable law, this may be treated as sensitive personal information.

We use this information only to build meal plans that respect your household’s needs, flag possible conflicts, and provide support. We never use allergy or health-related dietary information for advertising or sponsored-product targeting.

4. How We Use Information

  • Generate meal plans, replacement meals, and grocery lists
  • Mark ingredients you already have and suggest using food before its use-by date
  • Check that recipe links work and find working ones
  • Show your meal history and past meals
  • Send account emails, such as email verification and password reset
  • Provide customer support and keep the Service secure
  • Understand whether the Service is working and improve it

5. AI Processing (OpenAI)

DinnerNavAI uses OpenAI, through its business API, to generate meal plans and replacement meals and to look up recipe links. We only send your information to OpenAI after you tap “Allow” on the AI permission screen in the app, which appears before your first meal plan or replacement.

When you request a meal plan or replacement, we send OpenAI: household members’ first names and age groups; allergies, foods to avoid, and dietary patterns; eating styles, protein preferences, and favorite and rejected meals; your planning schedule and cooking time limits; and your kitchen inventory items. Recipe-link lookups send only meal titles. We do not send your email address, password, or other account credentials.

OpenAI processes this information to return results to us. Under OpenAI’s API terms, data sent through its API is not used to train OpenAI’s models by default, and OpenAI may retain it for a limited period (currently up to 30 days) to detect abuse before deleting it. See OpenAI’s privacy policy.

You can withdraw permission at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy and Data > AI Meal Planning. After you withdraw, we send nothing further to OpenAI, and the app can no longer generate new meal plans or replacements until you allow it again. Plans already generated stay in your account.

AI-generated meals can contain mistakes. Always check ingredients against your household’s allergies and dietary needs.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Service, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • OpenAI — AI meal-plan generation, as described in Section 5 (only with your permission)
  • Supabase — account sign-in and database storage
  • Render — hosting for our servers
  • Resend — sending account emails (your email address and the email content only)
  • Apple — app distribution through the App Store, under Apple’s own privacy policy

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights or safety of our users or others, or as part of a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your information.

Affiliate retailers and sponsors never receive identifiable household, inventory, allergy, health-related dietary, or meal-preference information. See our Affiliate & Sponsored Disclosure.

7. Data Retention

We keep your information while your account is open. Meal history older than 18 months is hidden in the app. When you delete your account, we delete your account and all of its household, inventory, meal-plan, and feedback data from our database. Copies in our providers’ backups and logs are removed on their normal schedules, and we may keep limited records when the law requires it.

8. Data Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Database access is restricted so that each account can read only its own data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

  • Delete your account in the app: Settings > Privacy and Data > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and its data.
  • Delete household data without closing your account: Settings > Privacy and Data > Delete Household Data.
  • Turn off AI sharing: Settings > Privacy and Data > AI Meal Planning.
  • View and edit your information anytime in the app.

Depending on where you live (for example, California under the CCPA/CPRA, or Iowa under its consumer data protection law), you may also have rights to confirm processing, get a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, opt out of certain processing, and appeal our decision on a request. To make a request, email us at the address below. We will verify and respond as applicable law requires.

10. Children's Privacy

DinnerNavAI accounts are for adults. Children under 13 may not create accounts or provide personal information to DinnerNavAI directly. A parent or legal guardian may enter limited information about a child, such as a first name, age group, allergies, and food preferences, through the parent’s own account so household meal plans can account for that child. If we learn that a child under 13 created an account or submitted personal information directly, we will delete it. Contact us immediately if you believe this has happened.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time and will post the updated policy with a new date. If we make material changes — for example, sharing your information with a new AI provider or for a new purpose — we will notify you in the app and ask for your permission again where required, rather than applying the change silently to information already collected.
